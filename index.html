Five minutes tonight, curls in the morning

Scroll to wrap, sleep and unwrap.

Step 1

Start with damp hair

Towel-dry until it is damp, not dripping, and brush out every tangle. Dry hair works too; damp hair sets a firmer curl.

Step 2

Place the rod

Lay the middle of the rod over the crown of your head so both ends hang evenly, and hold it with the claw clip.

Step 3

Wrap away from your face

Take a section from the front, twist it around the rod, then keep adding hair to each turn, like a rope braid, until one side is done. Repeat on the other side.

Step 4

Secure the ends

Slip a satin scrunchie over each end. Bend the rod tips up if you like; the wire core holds the shape. Take the clip out.

Step 5

Sleep on it

Leave it in for two hours or overnight. The soft foam core is comfortable to sleep on; back sleepers get the neatest result.

Step 6

Unwrap and shake out

Remove the scrunchies, slide the rod out from the top, and loosen the curls with your fingers. Tighter turns give curls, looser turns give waves.

Will it work on my hair?

The rod needs enough length to wrap at least two turns. It works best from shoulder length down.

Tips for the best curls

  • Damp, not wetVery wet hair may not dry overnight and the curl will drop.
  • Smaller sections, tighter curlsUse more, thinner sections for defined curls; fewer, wider ones for loose waves.
  • Add a light leave-inA little leave-in conditioner or mousse before wrapping helps the curl hold all day.
  • Sleep on satinA satin pillowcase keeps the outer layer of hair smooth overnight.

Questions

How long do the curls last?

Usually all day. Thick, heavy or very straight hair can relax faster; a little mousse before wrapping helps.

Is it comfortable to sleep in?

The rod has a soft foam core and a smooth satin cover, so there is nothing hard to lie on. Most people find it easiest sleeping on their back.

Can I use it on dry hair?

Yes. Lightly mist dry hair with water or a styling spray before wrapping and leave it in for a few hours.

Does it work on short hair?

It needs hair that reaches at least the shoulders to wrap around the rod. Chin-length hair is usually too short.

How do I clean it?

Hand-wash the rod and scrunchies in lukewarm water with a mild shampoo and let them air-dry. Do not tumble dry.
